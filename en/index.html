FAQ

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Social media packages are month to month. Cancel anytime with no penalty; we just ask for 5 days’ notice before your next payment.

How does the included ad spend work?

In the Starter, Growth and Pro packages, part of your monthly payment goes straight into Facebook and Instagram ads. We set up and optimize the campaign to reach as many customers as possible in your city. Want to spend more on ads? We add it.

Do you need my Facebook and Instagram passwords?

No. The safest way is to add us as editors or advertisers in Meta Business Suite. We guide you step by step.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer (SPEI), OXXO deposit and debit or credit card. Payment is monthly, at the start of each period. If your business is in the U.S., ask us about paying in dollars.

Does the package include a logo?

Packages cover social media content. If you need a logo or brand identity, we quote it separately.

Do you work with businesses outside Juárez?

Yes. Social media packages and websites are done 100% online for businesses in El Paso, Texas, Ciudad Juárez and anywhere in Mexico. We work in English or Spanish.

Do you ship printed products?

No. Coroplast cutouts, banners, stickers and all printed work are picked up at our shop: Calz. del Río 10060, Jardines de Coyoacán, Cd. Juárez.

Why pay if I have Canva?

Canva is a tool, not a strategist. We apply color psychology, composition and visual hierarchy based on your business goals. A generic template won’t help you stand out in a crowded market.

How long does it take?

Flyers and posts take 2 to 4 business days. A full brand identity takes 7 to 12 days depending on complexity. Digital Launch Pack websites are delivered in 72 hours. We always agree on a timeline before we start.

Do I get the editable files?

Yes. Logo and identity packages include vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) and high-resolution versions. The brand is 100% yours.

I don’t have a design to print. Can you help?

Sure. If you don’t have a design, we create it for an extra fee and make it print-ready.

What does the website renewal include?

After the first year, the annual renewal is $2,400 MXN and covers domain, hosting and SSL so your site stays online.
